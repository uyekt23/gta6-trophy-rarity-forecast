# GTA VI Trophy Rarity Forecaster

Forecasts, for every Grand Theft Auto VI PS5 trophy, five quantiles of its Exophase earn rate one week after launch. It reads the recorded history of the game's trophy page, compresses it into distinct page states, fits each trophy's logit earn rate against log-time with shrinkage toward its trophy kind, and emits honest wide quantile bands that account for Exophase's step refreshes and the fast-growing tracked-player pool.
